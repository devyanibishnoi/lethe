Benchmarks

Read live from docs/benchmark_results.csv, every historical run.

Deletion latency

How long one hard-delete takes, and how long a full cascading erasure (every document a subject has) takes. This is the actual cost of "forget me" at request time, not a projection.

Index rebuild cost vs. corpus size

Each deletion rebuilds only the affected tenant's index partition, never the whole dataset. This shows that cost at increasing corpus sizes, proof the partitioning strategy is actually doing its job, not just claimed in the design.

Recall@k, before vs. after deletion

After deleting a batch of documents, the same 20 queries are re-run and compared to their results before the deletion. A value near 1.0 means erasing one person's data didn't quietly degrade search quality for everyone else's.
